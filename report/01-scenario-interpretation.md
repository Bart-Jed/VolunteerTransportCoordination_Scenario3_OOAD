# What it must include:
- Organisation
	- Kerry Community Link coordinates non-emergency journeys for residents who have difficulty using ordinary public transport
	- Journeys include community activities, shopping, education, social appointments and essential local services
	- Volunteer drivers use their own cars, while partner organisations occasionally provide accessible vehicles
- Present Process
	- Drivers use their own cars, unless provided through partners
	- Completed journeys recorded through notes, email, spreadsheets and weekly schedules (printed).
	- Different coordinators may describe the same destination or passenger need differently, and changes are not always reflected on every copy
